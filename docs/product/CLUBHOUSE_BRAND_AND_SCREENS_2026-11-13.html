1 · Where we are

What the main screens do today

Checked on the live site and on a local copy at phone size (375 pixels wide), plus the screen code. The first two phones are redrawn from what is on screen. The third is redrawn from the code, because the demo account has no clubhouse to open. Mockups on this page are about three-quarter size.

First screen and welcome

  • 1No promise. The first screen is a logo and a form. A friend who taps an invite has no idea what they are joining. J-21
  • 2"Create Account" breaks onto two lines at phone width, so the two tabs sit at different heights.
  • 3Two voices. This screen shouts in spaced capitals (SIGN IN, FORGOT PASSWORD?). The rest of the app speaks in normal sentence case.
  • +Too many questions before any fun. Sign-up asks for four fields including the password twice, then an email confirmation, then a six-step welcome whose first step asks for the manager name again. J-06J-12
  • +Two main button colours. Sign In is blue. The welcome's Continue is gold. A person learns "the coloured button moves me forward" only if it is always the same colour.

Home and navigation

  • 4The screen explains itself. A title called Home plus a paragraph describing what Home is for. A good home screen needs no caption.
  • 5Three equal doors. Create, join by code, search. A newcomer has to stop and choose. One main door, two quiet ones.
  • 6Five tabs, two for the same thing. "Bets" opens My Bets and "Coins" opens the challenges screen. The label and the destination do not match. J-07J-08
  • +It counts instead of asking. "1 Clubhouse, 3 Competitions" is an inventory. The returning member wants to know what is waiting for them. J-14

Clubhouse and bets

  • 7Five tabs in a strip that scrolls, six for the owner. Digest leaves the screen entirely. Chat is fourth. C-03C-07
  • 8Insider words: P2P Challenges, Meta Rank, escrow. The cards also scroll sideways, so the third is cut off. C-04J-18
  • 9Activity is about competitions, not people, and sits at the very bottom. C-09
  • +A bet is made somewhere else. To bet a friend you leave the room, open another tab and fill a long form. The bet should be made where the argument happens. C-08J-11
  • +Emoji stand in for icons (ball, flag, speech bubble on the Invite button). They render differently on every phone. C-06

What is already good

  • ✓The light "paper" look is calm and readable. Colours were already adjusted to pass contrast checks.
  • ✓Touch targets are a proper 44 pixels and type has a 12 pixel floor.
  • ✓The newspaper is a real asset. Nothing else in this market writes a paper about your own friends.
  • ✓The sport badges are a system: every sport drawn from above, with one gold dot. The new logo can join that family.
2 · The name

Clubhouse is the platform, and the place your group meets

The product currently answers to four names. The web address says WC Fantasy Football. An old brand file and some welcome and invite wording still say Forza Fantasy League. Invite codes start with FK, from FantasyKit. The logo says Frontrow. One clean rename fixes all of them at once.

My first draft gave a group its own word, "Club". You said that overcomplicates it, and you are right. In the sports bar picture, Clubhouse is the bar. A group of friends has its clubhouse there: the space where everyone gets together. Inside it are leagues, one for each sport the group plays. Bets are made in the clubhouse, between its members, and do not belong to any one league. The app already calls a group's space a Clubhouse, so this keeps a word members know and removes the extra ones.

One writing rule

"Clubhouse" with a capital C is the product. "Your clubhouse" is your group's space. On screen, use the group's own name wherever there is one ("Friday Night Crew"), so the two never sit side by side and nobody has to think about which is meant.

Name check: deferred

You decided this is not critical now. It is logged as BRAND-4 so it happens before the app goes to the stores: whether the name can be registered for this kind of product, whether the stores accept the listing name, and which web address is free. A social audio app already uses the name.

4 · Design identity

Keep the base, fix the rules, give gold one job

The current identity (warm paper, navy ink, one blue, one gold, Archivo type) is sound and recently cleaned up. It does not need replacing. What it lacks is a small set of rules that every screen follows, and a little warmth. A rename is the right moment to set them.

VerdictItemWhat I found, and what to do
KeepPaper background and navy inkCalm, readable, different from the dark neon look of most betting and fantasy apps. It suits "a room with friends".
KeepArchivo Black and ArchivoStrong headlines, friendly body text, already loaded. The wordmark keeps the same letterforms.
KeepSport badges drawn from above, gold dotA real system. The Seat logo is the same idea applied to a table.
FixOne colour for the main buttonToday it is blue on sign-in and gold in the welcome. Proposal: main buttons are navy ink everywhere. Blue is for links and selected items.
FixOne way of writing labelsSentence case everywhere. No spaced capitals on buttons. Small technical-looking type only for numbers and codes.
FixOne corner sizeThe sign-in card uses 6 pixel corners while cards elsewhere use 12. Use 12 for cards and fields, fully round for chips.
FixIcons, not emojiAlready listed as C-06. The mockups below use one simple line-icon set.
FixLeftovers from older brandsThe installed-app settings still carry the old black and cyan colours, so the app opens on a black splash before a cream screen. One old logo file still reads Forza Fantasy League on a dark background.
ChangeGold means "you"Gold currently marks captains, deadlines, warnings and midfielders. Give it one meaning in the social parts: your move, your seat, your winnings. Warnings get their own amber.
ChangeFewer typefacesFour families are loaded and a fifth (Georgia) is used for the paper. Keep Archivo Black and Archivo for the app, and one serif for the paper only.
ChangeFaces before figuresSocial screens lead with people's avatars and names, then numbers. Today the clubhouse header leads with "2 Sports, 3 Competitions".

Direction 2 · Not taken
Members' Club

The clubhouse taken literally: cream paper, baize green, brass. Bets are paper slips with a tear-off stub. The Club has a plaque and an honours board. It feels like a place with history, even on day one.

Fraunces, a soft serif, for headlines. Archivo stays for small text and buttons.

Good at: charm and belonging. The slip is a memorable object people will screenshot and share.
Work needed: medium. A new colour set, a new headline typeface and a slip component, applied across every screen.
Watch out for: it may read as older and more formal than your audience. The green also sits very close to the green already used to mean tennis and to draw football pitches.
Direction 3 · Dark look not taken
Matchday

The group chat is the app. Dark, loud type, live scores at the top. There is no separate Home: you open straight into your club, with a tray of what is waiting. Every bet becomes its own conversation.

Archivo Black, used very large. Two tabs and one big gold button.

Good at: energy on match days and a younger, messaging-app feel. The strongest "social" statement of the three.
Work needed: the most. Dark colour values exist in the stylesheet but no screen uses them yet, so every screen needs checking, including squad, market and draft. Navigation changes for everyone.
Watch out for: the last move from dark to light left many invisible elements that had to be found and fixed. Going back risks the same. It also looks like every other sports app.

8 · Next steps

Decisions taken, and what happens next

  1. Logo: A, The Seat. Decided. Producing the files (app icons, browser tab icon, share image) is backlog item BRAND-2.
  2. Direction: 1, The Table, redrawn. Decided. Light mode only. The one-sentence bet and the bet conversation come from Direction 3. Direction 2 is dropped.
  3. Words. Decided. Clubhouse is the platform and the place a group meets. Inside a clubhouse are leagues. Bets belong to the clubhouse, not to a league.
  4. Name check: deferred. Logged as BRAND-4, to be done before the app goes to the stores.
  5. League bets stay for now. The drawings show the end state, with all bets at clubhouse level. Bets placed inside a league stay where they are, at least until the end of this season, because users are used to them. When to remove them is still open (backlog C-20).
  6. Nothing is being built yet. The changes are in the backlog. When you say go, the suggested order is: the rename and logo files, the identity rules, the first screen and invite page, Home, the Room, the sentence bet, the bet conversation.

Nothing on this page has been built. These are drawings to decide from, each screen shown on a phone and on a computer.
