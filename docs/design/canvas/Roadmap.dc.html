The one-line version
Frontrow's design system is better than the product built on top of it. Nothing here calls for a redesign — the palette, the type pairing and the primitives are all sound, and the critical findings are a day of token edits. The work worth committing to is the lint rule and the conversion habit, because those are what stop the gap between system and product widening again.
Caveat
Signed-in screens were assessed from source rather than exercised live — I can't enter credentials. Findings 4–6 and 10–12 are counted across the whole codebase and hold regardless; anything about flow or state inside the app is unverified and deliberately absent.
