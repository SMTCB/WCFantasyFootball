Clubhouse · football league · Option C in detail

My team: your squad and the market on one screen

The league opens on your team. Everything you do with it happens on that same screen: look at this round’s points, change the captain, make a substitution, buy and sell players, play a chip, check who is injured. On a phone the extra tools slide up from the bottom. On a computer they open in a panel to the right of the pitch, so your team never leaves the screen.

Design lock · approved 2026-11-21

The pitch, the player icons, the club crests and the sheet that opens when you tap a player stay exactly as they are in the app today. Only where they sit and what you can do from them change.

The pitches drawn on this page are sketches to show placement. The real screens use today's pitch and player sheet, unchanged. New things, like the Replace button, are added in the sheet's existing style.

Where everything goes

Every option you have today keeps a home

Nothing is lost. The Squad and Market pages close, and everything on them moves into the league screen.

My team, when you open the league

  • Top to bottom on a phone: league header, transfer window banner, tabs, the budget strip, a needs-attention line (only when someone is injured or doubtful), then the pitch with this round’s points, the bench, the chips and this round’s matches.

Screen 6

Chips

  • Always in view, so nobody forgets they have them: a row of three under the pitch on a phone, a card in the right column on a computer. Each shows Ready, Active this round, or Used and when.
  • Tap a chip to read its rule and press Use. An active chip shows on the pitch (“Triple Captain on”) and can be cancelled until the round locks.
  • Joker opens the market sheet in a “pick your Joker” mode, which only lists players outside your squad.
  • Max Cap just switches on, because it picks the player itself when scores are final.
  • Chips the commissioner switched off are hidden, as today.

What this fixes

Why it will not feel like another app

One frame

League header, window banner and tabs never change. Sheets and panels open on top of the league, they do not replace it.

No back button

The “Back to League” bar and the second clubhouse switcher are gone, because you never leave.

One look

Rounded pills for every sub-view, and normal text colours for budget and transfers. The small-capitals PITCH / LIST row and the red figures are retired.

Fewer taps

Replacing an injured player today: Edit in Squad, Market, filter, search, Buy, Sell, Confirm. In Option C: tap him, Replace, Swap, Confirm.

How each session proves the design lock

Screenshots of the pitch and the player sheet are taken at phone and computer width before and after the change, and they must match. The pitch files themselves are not restyled. The player sheet is moved into its own file as a straight cut and paste.

A change that would alter how the pitch or a player looks stops and comes back to you first.

No database changes are needed. Buying, selling, captaincy and chips keep using the same server functions as today; only the screens change.
